## 1. What you are selling (30-second version)

**STARSPIN** is a subscription SaaS that turns every customer into a player:

**QR scan → complete steps (Google review, social follow, etc.) → spin the wheel → win a prize → you collect their email.**

The business gets **more Google reviews**, **more social followers**, **repeat visits**, and a **CRM list** — without hiring staff to chase customers.

**Price:** **34€/month** or **340€/year** (2 months free). **7-day free trial** at signup. No setup fee. Cancel anytime.

## 3. The customer journey (what happens on the phone)

Show this live on **starspin.cc** or a demo merchant page.

1. **Scan QR** (sticker on table, counter, mirror, receipt sleeve)
2. **Branded page** opens (their logo + colors)
3. **Step 1…N** — merchant-configured, e.g.:
   - Leave a **Google review** (opens Google Maps — customer writes review, comes back)
   - **Follow on Instagram / Facebook / TikTok**
   - **TripAdvisor** review
4. **Upload screenshot** of published Google review (proof for the merchant)
5. **Spin the wheel** — instant, fun
6. **Claim prize** — name + email (+ optional phone)
7. **Prize code** on screen + **email to customer**
8. **Email to merchant** — customer info, coupon code, screenshot link, CRM link

**Merchant dashboard:** configure journey, prizes, branding, download QR, see CRM, approve/reject review screenshots, stats.

## 10. Objections & responses

### Price & budget

**“34€ is too expensive.”**  
→ “That’s about **1€ per day**. One extra customer or one saved bad review pays for the month. Try **7 days free** — if you get zero reviews, cancel.”

**“We don’t have budget.”**  
→ “No setup fee, no contract. Trial is free. Annual is **340€** — you can expense it as marketing.”

**“Can you do a discount?”**  
→ “Price is fixed on the site — **annual saves 2 months**. The trial lets you prove ROI before paying.”

---

### Trust & product

**“Does this actually work?”**  
→ “Customers complete steps because there’s a **prize at the end**. Many businesses see **first reviews same day**. You track everything in the dashboard.”

**“My customers won’t do it.”**  
→ “They already use their phone at the table. This takes **2–3 minutes** and they **win something**. Make the prize something they want — free drink, 10% off.”

**“Is this legal / a lottery?”**  
→ “You’re running a **promotional game** for customers who already bought something — not a random lottery. You set the prizes and rules. They should check local promo laws; we’re a **software tool**, not legal advisors.”

**“What if people cheat?”**  
→ “**Spin cooldown** per device, **screenshot proof** for reviews, **prize codes** you verify at the counter. You can reject fake screenshots in the dashboard.”

**“Can you prove they left a review?”**  
→ “Honestly: **Google doesn’t give APIs to prove one person reviewed.** We use **screenshot upload** + **you approve** in the dashboard — same as every competitor in this space. You see the screenshot before honoring the code.”

**“What if they fake the screenshot?”**  
→ “You see it in **Dashboard → Reviews**. Reject it — they don’t get a valid code honored. Most customers don’t bother faking for a free coffee.”

---

### Technical & setup

**“I’m not technical.”**  
→ “**10 minutes**: paste your Google link, add prizes, download QR. We handle the rest. I can set it up with you on your phone.”

**“What link do I paste for Google?”**  
→ “Anything from Google Maps — **Share → Ask for reviews**, or even a **share.google** link. **No Place ID needed** — we find your business automatically.”

**“Do I need an app?”**  
→ “**No app for customers.** Browser only. **No app for you** — everything in the dashboard on starspin.cc.”

**“What if the internet goes down?”**  
→ “Customer needs phone data/Wi‑Fi to play — same as paying by card. Your dashboard works when you’re online.”

---

### Google & reviews

**“Will Google ban me for incentivized reviews?”**  
→ “Google’s policy discourages **paying only for positive reviews**. Frame it as: **‘Leave an honest review, spin for a thank-you gift.’** Don’t say ‘5 stars = prize.’ Merchant responsibility — we recommend honest-review wording.”

**“We already have 4.8 stars.”**  
→ “Great — **fresh reviews** keep you on top. This also builds **email list** and **repeat visits**, not just stars.”

**“Bad reviews will increase too.”**  
→ “Customers who hate you won’t scan a QR. You’re engaging **happy** in-venue customers. You approve screenshots anyway.”

---

### Competition & alternatives

**“We use a loyalty stamp card.”**  
→ “Stamp cards don’t get **Google reviews** or **emails**. STARSPIN does reviews + loyalty in one.”

**“We run Instagram ads.”**  
→ “Ads cost more than 34€/day. This captures people **already in your shop** — highest intent.”

**“TripAdvisor is enough.”**  
→ “Add TripAdvisor as a **step** in the journey alongside Google — you configure both.”

**“I’ll build it myself.”**  
→ “You could — but QR, wheel, CRM, emails, abuse protection, hosting = months. **34€/mo** vs dev time.”

---

### Time & commitment

**“I don’t have time.”**  
→ “**15-minute setup once.** After that it runs itself. You only check dashboard when someone wins — or when you export emails.”

**“I need to think about it.”**  
→ “Start the **free trial** today — no charge for 7 days. Cancel in two clicks if it’s not for you. What’s the best email for the signup link?”

**“Talk to my partner.”**  
→ “Send them **starspin.cc** and this one-liner: ‘QR game for Google reviews, 7-day free trial.’ I’ll follow up [day].”

---

### Subscription & billing

**“Will I be charged automatically?”**  
→ “After **7-day trial**, yes — unless you cancel in **Dashboard → Billing**. Stripe sends receipts.”

**“Can I cancel anytime?”**  
→ “**Yes.** Cancel at period end — wheel stays live until then. Export CRM first if you want.”

**“What if I stop paying?”**  
→ “Public wheel **deactivates** until subscription is active again. Your data stays accessible for export while account exists.”

| “We’ll run it for you daily” | “Self-serve dashboard; support at hello@starspin.cc” |
