## READ THIS FIRST — What is STARSPIN?

STARSPIN is an **online tool** (website) that helps local businesses get:

- **More Google reviews** (better ranking on Google Maps)
- **More followers** on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
- **Customer emails** (for newsletters and promotions)
- **Repeat visits** (customers come back to use their prize)

**How?** The customer scans a **QR code** (square barcode sticker) on the table or counter. A fun **prize wheel** appears on their phone. Before they spin, they do simple steps — usually leave a Google review. They win a small prize (free drink, 10% off, etc.). The business owner gets their **email** and a **notification**.

**You are NOT selling software.** You are selling: **more reviews + more customers + more emails — on autopilot.**

## TOP 10 SELLING POINTS (advantages)

Use these in every conversation. Pick 3–4 that fit the business.

### 1. More Google reviews — without staff asking
**Problem:** Waiters forget to ask. Owners feel awkward.  
**STARSPIN:** Customer does it alone on their phone because they want to spin the wheel.  
**Say:** *"Your team stops chasing people. The QR does the work while they wait for the bill."*

### 2. Costs less than one ad campaign
**Problem:** Facebook/Google ads cost hundreds per month.  
**STARSPIN:** 34€/month — fixed price, unlimited customers.  
**Say:** *"One extra customer per week pays for the whole month."*

### 3. You collect customer emails legally
**Problem:** Businesses have no email list for promotions.  
**STARSPIN:** Every winner gives their email to claim the prize.  
**Say:** *"You build a contact list automatically — download it and send 'come back this weekend' emails."*

### 4. Customers come back (loyalty)
**Problem:** One-time visitors never return.  
**STARSPIN:** Prizes like "10% off next visit" or "free dessert next time."  
**Say:** *"The prize brings them back — not just a one-time discount."*

### 5. Setup in 10 minutes — no technician
**Problem:** Owners fear complicated tech.  
**STARSPIN:** Sign up online, paste Google link, add prizes, print QR.  
**Say:** *"I can set it up with you on your phone in 10 minutes — no IT guy needed."*

### 6. Your brand, not ours
**Problem:** Generic loyalty apps look cheap.  
**STARSPIN:** Their logo, their colors, their page.  
**Say:** *"Customers see YOUR brand — looks professional, like you built it."*

### 7. You control the prizes and costs
**Problem:** Afraid of giving away too much.  
**STARSPIN:** They choose prizes (free coffee, 10% off), odds, and stock limits.  
**Say:** *"You decide what's on the wheel — small prizes, big impact."*

### 8. Protection against cheaters
**Problem:** People might fake reviews or spin 20 times.  
**STARSPIN:** Screenshot check + limit how often same phone can play.  
**Say:** *"You see the review photo before honoring the prize. Same person can't abuse it daily."*

### 9. Owner gets an email every time someone wins
**Problem:** Owner doesn't know what's happening.  
**STARSPIN:** Email with customer name, email, prize code, review photo.  
**Say:** *"Every win — you get an email. Name, email, coupon code, review screenshot. Nothing hidden."*

### 10. Free trial — zero risk
**Problem:** "I don't want to pay for something that doesn't work."  
**STARSPIN:** 7 days free, cancel in 2 clicks.  
**Say:** *"Try it free for a week. If you get zero reviews, cancel — you lose nothing."*

## WHAT HAPPENS — Step by step (show on your phone)

1. Customer **scans QR sticker** on table  
2. **Their phone** opens a page with the shop's logo  
3. **Step 1:** "Leave a Google review" — opens Google Maps, they write review, come back  
4. **Step 2:** Upload **photo of review** (proof)  
5. **Step 3:** **Spin the wheel** — fun animation  
6. **Step 4:** Enter **name + email** to get prize  
7. Customer sees **prize code** on screen + gets **email**  
8. **Owner gets email too** — customer info + code + review photo  

**At the counter:** Customer shows code → staff gives prize (free drink, discount, etc.)

### "Too expensive"
*"34€ is about 1€ per day. One extra customer per week covers it. Try 7 days free — if it doesn't work, cancel."*

### "My customers won't do it"
*"They already use phones at the table. They spin for a FREE DRINK — takes 2 minutes. Make the prize something they want."*

### "I'm not good with computers"
*"No app to install. Sign up on starspin.cc — paste your Google link, add prizes, print QR. I'll do it WITH you in 10 minutes."*

### "What Google link do I need?"
*"Any link from Google Maps — Share button, 'Ask for reviews'. Even a short share.google link. We find your business automatically. No technical ID needed."*

*"They upload a photo of their review. YOU see it in your dashboard and approve it. You don't give the prize to fakes."*

*"It's a thank-you gift for leaving an HONEST review — not 'pay for 5 stars'. Say 'leave your honest opinion, spin for a gift.' Don't promise money for stars only."*

### "We already have good reviews"
*"Fresh reviews keep you on top. Plus you get EMAILS and repeat visits — not just stars."*

### "I need to think"
*"Start the free trial today — zero charge for 7 days. Cancel anytime. What's your email? I'll send the link."*

### "Can you give a discount?"
*"Price is on the website. Yearly plan saves 2 months. The free trial lets you prove it works first."*

### "What if I cancel?"
*"Cancel in dashboard — wheel stops at end of month. Export your email list first if you want to keep contacts."*
